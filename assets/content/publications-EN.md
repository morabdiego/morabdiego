## Nationwide Surveys & Research Projects
- **Household Debt in Low-Income Neighborhoods: Informal Credit Circuits & Non-Monetary Costs** (2026). Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa) & Friedrich Ebert Foundation (FES). Quota sample of 643 people across 12 neighborhoods in 7 provinces. [→ Report PDF](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/media/encuesta-endeudamiento-2026-v5.pdf) · [→ Project](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/proyecto/11)
- **Time-Use Survey in Low-Income Neighborhoods: Measuring Community Care Work** (2025–2026). Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa) & SAGE Foundation. 1,096 households and 1,241 people across 71 neighborhoods in 14 provinces. [→ Final Report PDF](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/media/EUT-TCC._Resultados_finales_2026.pdf) · [→ Open Dataset](https://github.com/ObservatorioVillero/observadata/tree/main/encuesta-uso-del-tiempo/2025) · [→ Project](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/proyecto/9)
- **Living Conditions in Low-Income Neighborhoods** (2024–2025). Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa), ACIJ, IIGG-UBA & Fundación TEMAS. 1,485 households and 5,024 residents across 9 neighborhoods in 6 provinces. [→ Report PDF](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/media/observatorio-villero_condiciones_de_vida_2024.pdf) · [→ Interactive Web](https://lapoderosa.acij.org.ar/) · [→ Open Dataset](https://github.com/ObservatorioVillero/observadata/tree/main/condiciones-de-vida/2024)
- **The Poverty Situation in Low-Income Neighborhoods: A Qualitative Study** (2023). Dávolos, P. — UNICEF Argentina & Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa). [→ Report PDF](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/media/Estudio_cualitativo__la_situaci%C3%B3n_de_la_pobreza_en_barrios_populares.pdf) · [→ Project](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/proyecto/7)
- **Housing Conditions and Public Services in Low-Income Neighborhoods** (2022). Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa), Rosa Luxemburg Foundation & El Gato y La Caja. 1,222 dwellings in 23 neighborhoods across 16 provinces. [→ Interactive Web](https://lapoderosa.elgatoylacaja.com/) · [→ Project](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/proyecto/6)
- **National Survey on Menstrual Health Management in Low-Income Neighborhoods** (2021). Socioeconomic Observatory (La Poderosa). 2,478 households and 3,598 menstruating people in 67 neighborhoods across 22 provinces. [→ Project](https://observatoriovillero.lapoderosa.org.ar/proyecto/1)
